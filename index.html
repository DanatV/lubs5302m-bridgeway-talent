THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Improving recruitment and selection

Bridgeway Talent is a recruitment agency serving 120 business clients. Its 48 consultants face a database of 50,000 candidate profiles and around 3,000 new or updated records each month. With the volume of candidates rising, the company is looking for data-driven solutions to improve selection of candidates and increase success rate with their clients.
